Computer Engineer · M.S. Data Science Researcher

Maryville University · St. Louis, Missouri

I am a Computer Engineer and M.S. Data Science researcher interested in trustworthy, explainable, and generalizable machine learning. My work spans healthcare AI, computer vision, infrastructure analytics, geospatial data, and disaster resilience.

I am particularly interested in understanding how machine learning systems behave under distribution shift, whether evaluation protocols accurately reflect real-world performance, and whether models make predictions using meaningful signals rather than spurious correlations.
